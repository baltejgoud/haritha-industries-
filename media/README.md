# Asset provenance

Company sources: https://harithaindustries.com/

- `brand-mark.png`: transparent padding trimmed from the original `logo.png`, preserving the mark.
- `clientlogos.png`: the company's published client-logo strip.
- `product-1.webp` through `product-7.webp`: the original `Proimg1.png` through `Proimg7.png`, cropped to remove the titles baked into the images. Their order matches the original product list: pressure vessels, cladded pressure vessels, heat exchangers, U-tube heat exchangers, reactors, pressure pipings and miscellaneous.
- `slideimg1.webp` through `slideimg3.webp`: resized versions of the company's gallery images (`slideimg3` has a white band trimmed from its bottom edge).
- `hero-workshop.webp`, `hero-workshop-1000.webp`: a detail crop of `slideimg1`.
- `certificate1.png`: historical BHEL recognition.
- `certificate2.png`: historical ISO 9001:2015 certificate.
- `certificate3.png`: historical boiler-component manufacturer registration.
- `certificate4.png`: historical boiler erector / repairer registration.
- `certificate*-thumb.webp`: small previews of the certificates above.
- `company-profile.pdf`: the original published company profile.
- `financials.pdf`: the original published financial document. It is not asserted to be the latest reporting period.

The full-size gallery and product originals are kept locally under `.design/raw-assets/` and are not part of the public build.
