How a project runs

  1. 01

    Understand & design

    We start with the application, operating conditions and your technical brief, and turn the requirement into an equipment design with the right materials and configuration.

    Requirement → engineered design
  2. 02

    Source & prepare

    Material selection and procurement follow the approved design. Components and the fabrication plan are prepared around the project requirements.

    Materials → fabrication readiness
  3. 03

    Fabricate & assemble

    Forming, welding and assembly, with attention to fit, finish and the details that make the equipment work.

    Drawings → finished equipment
  4. 04

    Inspect & deliver

    The completed equipment is reviewed against the agreed inspection and documentation requirements before dispatch.

    Final checks → delivery
